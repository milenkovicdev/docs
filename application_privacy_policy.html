Introduction

Your privacy is very important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how applications developed by Stefan Milenkovic ("Developer") handle information when you use them. Please read this document carefully to understand our privacy practices.

1. Who We Are

The applications covered by this Privacy Policy are developed by Stefan Milenkovic (referred to as "we" or "us"). These applications are designed to provide their intended features without requiring users to create an account.

2. Data We Collect

The applications covered by this Privacy Policy do not collect personal data such as your name, email address, phone number, or other information that directly identifies you.

We do not collect location data or use your personal information for tracking or advertising purposes.

3. Data Storage and Synchronization

Data created or entered within an application may be stored locally on your device to provide its features. Where an application supports synchronization through iCloud, you may choose to enable synchronization through your device settings.

  • Local Storage: Data generated by an application is stored on your device and is not sent to servers operated by the Developer.
  • iCloud Synchronization: If supported and enabled by you, application data may be synchronized through your personal iCloud account so that it can be available across your compatible Apple devices. The Developer does not have access to or control over your personal iCloud data.

4. Data Sharing with Third Parties

The applications covered by this Privacy Policy do not share your personal data with third parties for advertising, tracking, or marketing purposes.

Some applications may use services provided by Apple, such as iCloud or the App Store. These services are operated by Apple and are subject to Apple's own terms and privacy policies.

When you access this Privacy Policy or other external content hosted on third-party platforms, such as GitHub Pages, those platforms may collect information independently of us. We do not control or take responsibility for the privacy practices of third-party platforms.

5. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the applications and the data they store. Data stored locally remains on your device, while synchronization through iCloud is managed by Apple using Apple's security and encryption mechanisms.
